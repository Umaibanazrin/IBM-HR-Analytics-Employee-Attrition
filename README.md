# IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and performance using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The objective is to identify the major factors influencing employee attrition and provide data-driven recommendations to improve employee retention.

## 📊 Dataset

- Employees: 1,470
- Features: 35
- Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
- Target: Employee Attrition

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Tableau Desktop
- Microsoft Excel

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The project analyzes employee attrition based on:

- Department
- Age and Monthly Income
- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Training
- Education
- Years at Company

## 📈 Key Findings

- Overall employee attrition rate is 16.12%.
- Sales has the highest attrition rate at 20.63%.
- Younger and lower-income employees show higher attrition.
- Employees working overtime have a 30.5% attrition rate compared with 10.4% for employees without overtime.
- Lower job satisfaction and insufficient training are associated with higher attrition.
- The first year has the highest attrition risk, accounting for approximately 25% of exits.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Implement an overtime control program.
2. Introduce a structured first-year employee experience program.
3. Improve career development and training opportunities.
4. Review compensation for high-risk employee groups.
5. Develop targeted retention strategies for high-attrition departments.

## 🎯 Project Outcome

The analysis provides actionable insights into employee attrition and helps identify areas where HR teams can focus their retention efforts.
